🔹 Expenses Management
Two Types of Expenses:
1.	Dukaan Kharcha Khata (Shop Expenses)
o	Tea for staff, Disposable Glasses, VIP Water, Cleaning, etc.
o	Expense Description from Admin’s dropdown list.
o	Amount (₹)
o	Auto Date & Time Capture
2.	Commission Kharcha Khata (Commission Expenses)
o	Example: Admin sets rate 1200, Salesman sells at 1000 → 200 Commission Loss.
o	Record: Amount, Description (auto from transaction), Date-Time.
